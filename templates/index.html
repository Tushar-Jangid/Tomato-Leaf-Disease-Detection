Agronomic Risk Advisories

Current seasonal vulnerability & preventive management guide.

High Risk

Late Blight (Phytophthora)

High humidity (>80%) and wet leaves encourage rapid zoospore germination. Apply preventive copper spray prior to rainy spells.

Moderate Risk

Early Blight & Septoria Spot

Soil splash from heavy showers spreads fungal spores to bottom leaves. Mulch plants and prune lower 12 inches of foliage.

Vector Alert

Whiteflies & Spider Mites

Dry, sunny spells cause whitefly populations to surge, vectoring Yellow Leaf Curl Virus. Deploy yellow sticky cards immediately.
